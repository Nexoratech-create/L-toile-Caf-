OUR CRAFT & PASSION

Every Cup Tells a Story

At L'Étoile Café, coffee is more than a morning routine — it is an art form. Founded in 2021, our roastery sources direct-trade micro-lots from sustainable family farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala.

Our in-house roasters slow-roast each batch to unlock complex tasting notes, while our pastry chefs craft authentic French viennoiserie every morning before sunrise.
